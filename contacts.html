Contacts and reservation

↓Jump to opening hours

The easiest way to reach us is by phone, especially if you need a quick answer about a product or a reservation. If you prefer messaging over phone calls, you can also write to us on Instagram. We recommend calling if you need to check the availability of a specific item or size. During business hours, we try to respond to questions as quickly as possible.

Our store is located at 5/5 Ryskulov Street, Office 3. If you're getting here by public transport, the nearest stop is "City Hospital No. 2" — it's only a three-minute walk from there to the store. The entrance is directly from the street, so you won't need to search for it inside another building. Once you arrive, look for the storefront window and the glass door.

The store is open every day from 11:00 to 21:00, with no days off. This makes it easy to plan a visit — you can come by during the day or in the evening, after school or work. You can pick whatever time works best for you, without having to wait for the weekend.

We recommend reserving an item in advance, since our store is small and some items are only available in limited quantities. The model or size you like could be sold before you arrive. A reservation gives you the confidence that the item will be waiting for you. This is especially convenient if you already know your size and don't want to make a trip for nothing.
