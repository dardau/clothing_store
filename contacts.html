How to contact us

The easiest way to contact us is by phone if you need a quick answer about a product. You can also write to us on Instagram if you prefer messaging instead of calling. We recommend calling first when you need to check whether a particular item or size is available. During business hours, we try to answer questions as quickly as possible.

Where we are located

Our store is located at 5/5 Ryskulov Street, Office 3, near City Hospital No. 2. If you are coming by public transport, the hospital is a useful landmark, and the store is about three minutes away on foot. The entrance is from the street side, so you do not need to search for an entrance inside another building. Look for the storefront and glass door when you arrive.

Opening hours

The store is open every day from 11:00 to 21:00, so there are no regular days off. This makes it convenient to visit both during the day and in the evening after classes or work. You can choose a time that fits your schedule without waiting for the weekend.
