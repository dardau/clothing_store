Placing an Order

Ordering from Instyle.zh2 is simple and involves almost no extra steps. First, you message WhatsApp at +7 701 254 02 03 (the same link is in the Instagram profile header) or Instagram Direct @instyle.zh2 — this is usually where size, color, and availability of a specific model get confirmed. Once the manager confirms that the item is in stock and fits, the pickup or delivery method is arranged: pickup from one of three locations (Nexpo City residential complex, Respublika Avenue, or Severnoye Siyanie residential complex, open daily from 11:00 to 21:00) or courier delivery around Astana. Judging by the conversations the store publishes in its “reviews” story highlight, customers usually message the manager again after receiving their order to share their impression. Payment can be made in cash, by bank card, or via Kaspi QR right at the store; there’s no separate online fitting before payment — the decision is based on photos and measurements the seller sends. The store doesn’t have a separate website or shopping-cart catalog — the whole ordering process relies on messaging and personal contact with the sales consultant.
