Welcome

Instyle.zh2 is a small chain of women’s clothing boutiques in Astana, known on Instagram as @instyle.zh2. The store runs three locations across the city — in the Nexpo City residential complex, on Respublika Avenue, and in the Severnoye Siyanie residential complex — all open daily from 11:00 to 21:00. It’s run by Kusainov Temirlan Musaevich and has grown over several years from a single showroom into its current three-location chain. The store focuses on everyday women’s clothing at affordable prices — sweatshirts, dresses, trousers and accessories — with new arrivals posted to Instagram stories almost every week. Most communication with customers happens directly through WhatsApp and Instagram Direct, where shoppers get sizing advice before ordering. What sets it apart from bigger retail chains is this personal, conversational way of shopping rather than an anonymous storefront.
