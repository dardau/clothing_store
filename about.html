About Instyle.zh2

Everyday style, thoughtful service, and new collections in Astana.

View collection

Instyle.zh2 is a women’s clothing boutique with three locations in Astana. The stores are open daily from 11:00 to 21:00 and combine affordable everyday pieces with personal styling advice.
