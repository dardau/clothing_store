What categories are available

The Instyle.zh2 catalog covers a mix of everyday clothing, including jackets, skirts, long-sleeves, and T-shirts, so there's usually something for both casual and slightly dressier looks. Beyond clothing, the store also carries accessories like caps and bags, which means you can put together a full outfit without needing to shop somewhere else. The assortment stays fairly compact, but it's varied enough that browsing through it doesn't take long.

How often the assortment is updated

New arrivals show up fairly regularly, and the easiest way to keep track of them is through the store's Instagram stories rather than this page alone. Since the store doesn't have its own website, Instagram is really where everything gets announced first — new pieces, restocks, and sales all appear there before anywhere else. Checking it every so often is the best way to catch something before it sells out.

What makes the store different

One detail that stands out right away is the ZH2 branding — it's on the hangers, the tags, pretty much everywhere, which gives the store a more put-together feel than a typical small shop. What we liked is that it's not trying to be everything at once: the selection is small but genuinely varied, mixing basic everyday pieces with accessories, so you can look through several categories without feeling overwhelmed.

What to check before ordering

Because the store is small, it's worth double-checking the price, size, and current availability of an item before deciding on it — stock can shift quickly, and something popular might already be gone by the time you decide. The simplest way to confirm all of this is to message the store on WhatsApp directly rather than relying only on what's shown here.
